A complex world.
A clear experience.

End-to-end digital design for RAD, bringing technical content, brand expression and mobile into one visual language.

01 / THE CHALLENGE

Many kinds of content.
One coherent experience.

The project brought together a large website and mobile experience. My work connected the client’s requirements with a refreshed digital design language and a more modern presentation of communication products.

The design needed room for technical content, use cases and articles, while keeping a recognizable identity across the experience.

02 / DESIGN LANGUAGE

Technical content,
with a human presence.

I developed a visual language that connects the website’s content with the brand. Warm gradients, violet typography, flowing lines and rounded surfaces give the design a consistent foundation.

That language extends beyond the hero. In the homepage’s company section, photography and a process timeline bring people and production into the story.

DESIGN DECISION / DISCOVERY

Put search
in the first view.

The homepage pairs its brand message with a prominent search field. In the typing state, results appear directly beneath it and identify whether an item is a product, solution or blog entry.

This gives the visitor a direct route into the content alongside the main navigation and the homepage’s category cards.

03 / CONTENT DESIGN

Different content.
Different cues.

I gave use cases and articles distinct visual treatments while keeping the same underlying card rhythm.

Use cases use branded graphic covers and concise titles. Insights introduce imagery, resource icons and short summaries. Category labels help distinguish the content before a visitor opens it.

04 / RESPONSIVE DESIGN

The same identity.
A different rhythm.

The mobile homepage keeps the gradient, line work and search field, while the navigation becomes compact and the category cards stack vertically.

Typography and spacing change with the viewport. The brand remains recognizable, and the content has room to read.

THE DESIGN SCOPE

One connected visual language across website and mobile, with distinct content treatments and product communication.
