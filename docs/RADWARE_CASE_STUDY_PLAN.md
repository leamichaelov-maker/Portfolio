## Editorial direction

Tell the story of making a complex website coherent through end-to-end design. Use the reference's visual pacing: a short project introduction, the challenge, an early view of the solution, then individual design decisions with large supporting screens. Preserve Lea's portfolio identity and typography rather than copying the reference's branding.

The available project facts support website/mobile design, a digital design language, distinct treatment of use cases and articles, and modern communication-product visualization. Do not claim measured conversion gains, usability studies, a before/after baseline, project dates or business impact without evidence. The reference's metrics are not evidence for RADware.
